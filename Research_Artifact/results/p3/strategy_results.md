## 5b. How to read this (re-authored 2026-10-02 under DL-034)

> Authored text, added after the run. It introduces **no new number**; every figure is in §3–§5 above.
> This is the DL-034 test replay (completion-causal ④b history, `--rerun-under DL-034`). The original
> replay and its §5b are preserved at commit `1e38db7` and in `results/corrections/dl034/original/`.

- **The frozen policy saves carbon against static execution at a bounded delay.** ⑤ is
  −70.7 [−78.5, −64.9] gCO₂e per 1,000 builds against ① (−2.480%). It defers 7.92% of builds for at
  most 24 h, adding 1.017 h of mean latency across all builds and +12.24 h to failed-build TTFF p95.
- **Blanket carbon-aware scheduling saves more carbon at a much larger feedback cost.** ② saves
  4.681% against ①, but ⑤'s TTFF p95 is 113.11 h lower [−116.00, −110.90], at +62.7 gCO₂e per 1,000
  builds. Its deferred builds wait up to a week (latency p95 153.00 h).
- **Duration selectivity is what separates ⑤ from ③.** At +9.6 gCO₂e per 1,000 builds, ⑤ cuts TTFF
  p95 by 5.91 h and mean latency by 1.424 h.
- **④a and ④b at the same point are nearly indistinguishable.** ⑤ (≡ ④b) − ④a is −2.0 gCO₂e per
  1,000 builds, and its TTFF p95 CI spans 0 (−0.16 [−0.61, +0.18]).
- **⑥'s carbon comes from not running builds, and under DL-034 it overtakes ⑤ at mid τ.** At τ = 0.30
  it saves 15.205% but misses 7,054 failures (recall 0.7968). At τ = 0.20 and 0.25 it now saves *more*
  than ⑤ (5.471% and 6.457%) while missing 680 and 1,156 failures (recall 0.9804 and 0.9667). At
  τ = 0.15 the carbon difference from ⑤ is not significant (⑤ − ⑥ +4.3 [−6.9, +15.7]) at 219 missed
  failures. At τ ≤ 0.10 it saves less than ⑤ and still misses failures (44 and 75). This differs from
  the original replay, where every positive τ below 0.30 saved less than ⑤: ⑥ is driven by `p̂` from
  `xgboost:full`, whose refit under DL-034 selected different hyperparameters (P1-T5). ⑥ is a
  secondary comparison with no predeclared τ (DL-028 §3); its carbon is bought with unrun builds and
  missed failures, never comparable to deferral on carbon alone.
- **Gate safety holds on the test trace.** No non-deferrable build was deferred or skipped in
  14,146,686 records. As DL-020 §6 states, that shows consistency with the rule, not its correctness.
