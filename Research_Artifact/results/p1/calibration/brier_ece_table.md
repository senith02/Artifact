> ### ⚠ In-sample isotonic ECE is degenerate — read it as 0 by construction, not as perfect calibration

> Isotonic regression is fitted by pooling adjacent violators, and on the very data it was fitted to, the mean prediction inside each of its level sets equals the mean outcome there. When the predictions then fall inside a single 10-bin ECE cell, `conf(B_m)` and `acc(B_m)` coincide and ECE collapses to ~0 **arithmetically**.
>
> Affected arms (4 of 6): `logreg:control`, `random_forest:control`, `logreg:full`, `random_forest:full`.
>
> The out-of-fold column below is the meaningful calibration signal for these arms, and P3-T1's test-split ECE is the reportable one. An ECE of 0.000000 here must never be quoted as a calibration result.
